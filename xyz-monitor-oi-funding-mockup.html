Short answer

Yes — for OI and funding we already hold more history than the charts tab is drawing. Every ~4.5 minutes the poller writes [ts, oiBase, funding] per market to the append-only oi.log on the volume: full resolution for 31 days, hourly-thinned out to 365 days for xyz names, flat 31 days for crypto. That is the same spine the drawer sparklines and the ΔOI columns already read — it is simply not wired into a pane yet. Liquidations are a different story: they exist only for the crypto universe, only via Coinalyze, and only as CEX-aggregate data, so they stay labelled as context rather than as our book.

Proposed: studies stack under each pane

Not an overlay. OI in units and funding in APR share no scale with price, and a second y-axis on the price plot is the one chart mistake worth refusing outright — so each study gets its own strip beneath the candles, on the pane's existing time axis, with one shared crosshair. Toggle the strips in the toolbar; panes without a source say so instead of drawing a flat line. Hover any pane below.

mode panes studiesnew oi basis
price + volume: deep archive, bars verbatim from the exchange · open interest: oi.log samples bucketed to the pane timeframe, last sample in bucket · funding: time-weighted mean per bucket, shown as APR, band = the name's own 31d p10–p90 · liquidations: aggregated CEX (Coinalyze), crypto universe only · every pane states its own coverage on the line beneath it

Why it is drawn this way

Reading the strips

  • OI as an area, not a line. The question asked of OI is "is positioning building or unwinding through this move", which is a magnitude read against its own recent floor — an area answers it at a glance, and the endpoint dot marks the live sample.
  • Funding diverges around zero in amber (longs pay) and blue (shorts pay) rather than green/red. Positive funding is not "good"; borrowing the P&L palette for it would say something false, and it keeps the study readable for red-green CVD.
  • The p10–p90 band is what turns "+22% APR" into "richest this name has been all month". That percentile is already computed server-side as fundPct.
  • Liquidations stay mirrored — shorts up, longs down — matching the drawer exactly, so the two places never teach different conventions.

Honest gaps, drawn as gaps

  • OI history is shorter than price history. Candles reach back to each listing's birth; OI reaches 365d (xyz) or 31d (crypto). Zoom past coverage and the strip ends — it does not extrapolate. The coverage line says which.
  • Sub-5-minute panes cannot resolve OI. Sampling is ~4.5 min, so a 5m pane is at the sampling floor and anything finer would be interpolation. The strip greys out below 5m rather than inventing bars.
  • No liquidations for xyz names. HIP-3 equity perps have no Coinalyze counterpart, so LIQ shows "no CEX counterpart for this listing" — never an empty axis.
  • Crypto OI thins at 31d while xyz keeps a year. Same strip, different coverage; the label carries the difference.

What it would take

  • Storage: done. oi.log already holds it, pruning already keeps the right shape, and it reloads on boot.
  • Server: give /api/series the parameters a pane needs (tf, from, to) and bucket server-side — last sample in bucket for OI, time-weighted mean for funding — so the client renders and never re-derives, the same contract the derivs panel already follows.
  • Client: one study-region layout pass in chDraw, the toggles above in chBuild, and the study values folded into the existing hover readout. The crosshair, zoom, pan and LINK machinery all work unchanged.
  • Cache: panes fetch per (coin, tf, window); the ETag key folds the coin's existing spine stamp, so tf-toggle spam 304s.
